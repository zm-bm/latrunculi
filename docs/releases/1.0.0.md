## Verification

GCC and Clang release builds passed all 371 unit tests and randomized stress.
Clang ASan/UBSan and TSan builds passed the same suites. LeakSanitizer was run
outside `ptrace`.

The GCC and Clang executables both reported `Latrunculi 1.0.0`, the documented
Hash, Clear Hash, Threads, and Ponder options, `uciok`, and `readyok`, then shut
down normally.

## External Strength

The gauntlet used the candidate revision above and OpenBench revision
`00d55c4b2cefdb7a153bc960f921b9dc395aebc8` on an AMD Ryzen 7 2700X. Every
match used `10+0.1`, `Threads=1 Hash=32`, the `UHO_Lichess_4852_v1.epd` book,
no tablebases, and 400 cp for three moves and 10 cp for eight moves after move
40 as the win and draw adjudication. Time controls were normalized to 2,050,000
Latrunculi nps. OpenBench completed in-flight pairs after each 200-game target.

Reference ratings are from the [CCRL Blitz complete list][ccrl-blitz], computed
on August 28, 2026. Elo differences and 95% intervals are from OpenBench and
are rounded to whole numbers. Results are from Latrunculi's perspective.

| Opponent | CCRL Blitz | Test | Games | W-L-D | Score | Elo difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| [Weiss 0.9][weiss-09] | 2651 | #18 | 208 | 72-78-58 | 48.6% | -10 ± 37 |
| [4ku 2.0][4ku-20] | 2723 | #14 | 206 | 58-90-58 | 42.2% | -54 ± 38 |
| [Willow 2.8][willow-28] | 2842 | #15 | 206 | 62-90-54 | 43.2% | -48 ± 41 |

The Willow build configuration initially failed on one worker before play and
was then fixed. Willow also emitted invalid uppercase UCI promotion suffixes in
three games, which were credited to Latrunculi. Excluding them gives 59-90-54
and about -53 Elo, leaving the broad result unchanged. Latrunculi recorded no
crashes, time losses, or illegal moves in the gauntlet.

Together, the 620 games place Latrunculi at roughly 2700 in CCRL Blitz terms.
This is coarse context, not a directly transferable rating: CCRL uses different
hardware, openings, and testing conditions.

PGNs are stored by the OpenBench server as `Media/PGNs/14.pgn.tar`,
`Media/PGNs/15.pgn.tar`, and `Media/PGNs/18.pgn.tar`.

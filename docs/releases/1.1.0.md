# Latrunculi 1.1.0

Draft release notes — not yet published.

## Changes since 1.0.0

- Retuned the handcrafted evaluation and added terms for connected pawns.
- Refined search pruning and reductions, including aspiration-window retries,
  adaptive null-move reductions, and treatment of late quiet moves.
- Added time management that adjusts the search budget using information from
  completed iterations.
- Fixed checkmate handling at the fifty-move boundary and improved
  transposition-table handling near that limit.
- Reduced transposition-table memory use per entry and added prefetching and
  optional huge-page backing, with a fallback to ordinary allocation.

## Playing strength

In our OpenBench matches, the engine scored **about +195 Elo over 1.0.0**
across 2,004 games. Results against three other engines suggest **roughly
2830 CCRL Blitz equivalent**.

| Opponent | CCRL Blitz reference | Games | Wins–losses–draws | Score | Elo difference |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| [4ku 2.0][4ku] | 2723 | 2,002 | 990–524–488 | 61.6% | +82 |
| [Stash 25.0][stash] | 2933 | 2,006 | 564–979–463 | 39.7% | −73 |
| [Drofa 3.0.0][drofa] | 2898 | 2,006 | 501–982–523 | 38.0% | −85 |
| [Latrunculi 1.0.0][v1] | — | 2,004 | 1246–226–532 | 75.4% | +195 |

Results are from Latrunculi's perspective. The October 9, 2026 matches used
`10+0.1` with OpenBench speed normalization, one thread, 32 MiB hash, and no
tablebases on an AMD Ryzen 7 2700X. Openings came from
`UHO_Lichess_4852_v1.epd`, with colors reversed in paired games. Drofa needed
a [missing include added][drofa-fix] to build with GCC 15; its benchmark
results matched upstream.

The estimate fits a single Elo rating to the 6,014 games against external
opponents, using the [CCRL Blitz ratings][ccrl] published in the list computed
October 3, 2026. Each match's Elo difference is calculated from its score as
`400 × log10(score / (1 − score))`, with a draw worth half a point.

**This is an estimate from our own tests, not an official CCRL rating.**
Individual opponents imply ratings from about 2805 to 2860. Different testing
conditions and repeated openings limit precision; that spread is not a
confidence interval. These results do not translate directly to Lichess or
FIDE ratings.

## Testing and release status

The 1.1.0 build passed all 411 unit tests, randomized stress, and a UCI smoke
check. Its benchmark and all 200 fixed-depth search cases matched the
[tested engine revision][baseline]. That revision also passed ASan/UBSan and
focused TSan checks before the version bump.

The four completed matches covered 8,018 games with no reported crashes or
time losses.
